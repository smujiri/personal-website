Sophio Mujiri, Founder and Managing Partner of Mujiri IP, participated in the Annual Meeting of the International Trademark Association (INTA) 2026, held from 2 to 6 May in London, widely recognised as the world’s leading event for intellectual property professionals.

Bringing together more than 9,500 participants from over 140 jurisdictions, INTA 2026 served as a unique platform for public and private sector dialogue on major intellectual property issues, including developments in trademark law, artificial intelligence, online enforcement, brand protection, emerging technologies and international IP strategies.

During the event, Sophio Mujiri attended professional sessions, panel discussions and strategic meetings with international IP practitioners, partner firms, associates and business representatives from around the world. As the sector’s largest networking forum, INTA offered an exceptional opportunity to strengthen professional relationships, exchange practical experience and discuss future cooperation on cross-border IP matters.

Participation in INTA reflects a strong commitment to staying closely connected to the international IP community, following the latest global developments and identifying innovative approaches and new opportunities in the rapidly evolving intellectual property landscape.
